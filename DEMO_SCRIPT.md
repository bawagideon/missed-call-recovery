# Demo Script: Missed-Call Revenue Recovery

**Audience:** Practice Owners, Local Service Founders  
**Surface:** `projects/missed-call-recovery/public/index.html`

---

## 🎬 30–60 Second Walkthrough

1. **[00:00 - 00:15] The Reality:**
   *"When someone calls your clinic or business at 7:30 PM, what happens? The phone rings, nobody answers, they hear voicemail, they hang up, and they dial the competitor right below you on Google."*

2. **[00:15 - 00:30] The Live Simulation:**
   *"Let's simulate that right now. We trigger an unanswered call to Austin Dental Care."*
   *(Action: Click 'Simulate Unanswered Call (No-Answer)'.)*

3. **[00:30 - 00:45] The 4-Second Rescue:**
   *"In 3.4 seconds, watch the caller's phone screen. Instead of losing the patient, an automated, conversational text arrives immediately: 'Hi, thanks for calling Austin Dental Care! We're assisting another patient and missed you. If you need an urgent slot, grab it here in 1 click: [Priority Booking Link]'.*

4. **[00:45 - 00:60] The Payback:**
   *"The patient locks in their appointment in 30 seconds. You just saved an $850 appointment while your front desk was at home sleeping. That is Missed-Call Revenue Recovery."*
